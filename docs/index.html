Add it to your Home Screen

Two taps, and it opens like an app — full screen, no address bar.

  1. Open the link above in Safari (not Chrome — only Safari can install web apps on iPhone).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom — the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. AttendX now sits on your Home Screen with the other apps.
One thing you must do in person Your face has to be enrolled before the gate scanner can recognise you. A browser can't do that — visit the department office once and it's done. After that, everything shows up here.

How to install

  1. Tap the download button. Chrome will warn that this type of file can harm your device — that warning appears for every APK, not just this one. Tap Download anyway.
  2. When it finishes, tap Open in the notification, or find attendx-v1.2.5.apk in your Files app under Downloads.
  3. Android will say "For your security, your phone isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source." Tap Settings, then turn on Allow from this source, then press Back.
    Why this appears Android only trusts the Play Store by default. You're telling it to trust Chrome for this one install. You can turn the switch back off afterwards.
  4. Tap Install, and wait a few seconds.
  5. Play Protect may offer to scan the app. Let it — it takes a moment and comes back clean. If it says "unsafe app blocked", tap More details → Install anyway.
  6. Tap Open, sign in, and enroll your face when it asks.

If something goes wrong

It says "App not installed"

Almost always an older copy still on the phone. Uninstall AttendX from Settings → Apps, then install again. If you're updating and want to keep your login, make sure you're installing a newer version than the one you have — Android refuses to install an older one over a newer one.

"There was a problem parsing the package"

The download didn't finish. Delete the file from Downloads and download it again on a stable connection. This also happens on Android 7 and below, which this app doesn't support.

I can't find the downloaded file

Open the Files app (or My Files on Samsung) and look in Downloads. Or open Chrome, tap the three dots, and choose Downloads.

The camera doesn't open during face enrollment

Go to Settings → Apps → AttendX → Permissions and allow Camera. Then reopen the app and try again.

Prefer not to install anything? The web version works on Android too. It shows your attendance and timetable, but it can't enroll your face.

What you get

  • Attendance day by day, split into theory and lab
  • Subject-wise percentages, with a warning below 75%
  • Your timetable, including cancellations and room changes
  • A notification the same day if you're marked absent
  • Holidays and vacations already on the calendar

Face enrollment and the classroom scanner are Android-only — they need the camera and an on-device model that browsers don't provide.
